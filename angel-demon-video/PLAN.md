# Angel & Demon: video plan

A 9:16 vertical video, about 26 seconds long, built on the voiceover from video 5 (audio by **@Vincey**; credit them in the caption when you post).
The story beats follow the voiceover. The characters, setting, art style and gags are new.

- **You:** Maya, who grows angel wings.
- **Your friend:** Leo, who grows demon horns.
- **Ending gag:** at the cinema, Leo toasts the popcorn with a fingertip flame and Maya's halo works as a phone flashlight.

## 2. Google Flow image prompts

**How to generate:**
1. **Make the two character sheets first** (A and B). In Flow, add them as **ingredients** (reference images) for every shot they appear in. That keeps Maya and Leo looking the same across shots.
2. Set the aspect ratio to **9:16 (portrait)**.
3. Every prompt below already contains the shared style text, so you can paste each one as it is.
4. Pick the best result for each shot and save it as `shot01.png`, `shot02.png` and so on. The file names must match the shot numbers.

> **Shared style (already included below):** 2D cartoon comic illustration, bold clean black ink outlines, flat cel shading, expressive oversized eyes, vibrant saturated colors, vertical 9:16 portrait composition, no text, no letters, no captions, no watermark, keep the lower third of the image simple for captions.

## 3. What to send back
- Images `shot01.png` to `shot13.png` (plus `feather.png` if you made it). Portrait 9:16, ideally 1080×1920 or larger.
- **How to send them:** upload them to this repo on the `ccr-f7565108-2fqx5r` branch, in the folder `angel-demon-video/images/` (on GitHub: *Add file → Upload files*). You can also attach them here in the chat if your app lets you.
- **Optional:** any real sound-effect files you prefer (for example from Pixabay, as MP3/WAV). If you don't send any, I'll generate the sound effects myself.

## 4. What I'll do with them
- **Assembly with ffmpeg:** push-ins, pans, shakes, flashes, glow and flicker, and the floating feather, with each cut timed to the word timings above.
- **Captions:** burned in with a bold comic font, synced to the voiceover.
- **Audio:** the original voiceover with sound effects mixed underneath at the marked times.
- **Output:** a 1080×1920, 30 fps MP4 that's ready for TikTok.
