# Engagement Swords: video plan

A 9:16 vertical video, about 23 seconds long, built on the voiceover from this TikTok (audio by **Zac Speaks Giant**; credit them when you post).
The story beats follow the voiceover. The characters and gags are new, drawn in the same art style as our previous two videos.

- **Bjorn (the groom):** a short, stocky, soft-hearted Viking with a big blond braided beard.
- **Astrid (the bride):** a tall, fierce red-haired shieldmaiden.
- **Bjorn's late father:** a skeleton in a helmet who really doesn't want to let go of his sword.
