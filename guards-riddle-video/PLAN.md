# Two Guards Riddle: video plan

A 9:16 vertical video, about 15 seconds long, built on the voiceover from video 1 (audio by **Zac Speaks Giant**; credit them when you post).
The story beats follow the voiceover. The characters, setting and gags are new, drawn in the same art style as the Angel & Demon video.

- **Elara:** a brainy elf wizard who loves riddles.
- **Grunk:** a huge half-orc barbarian who doesn't.
- **The twin guards:** two identical, very shiny palace guards in front of a glowing magic door.

The violence stays cartoony: the guard gets flattened with X-eyes and his helmet goes flying. There's no blood.
