# Smooth Talker: video plan

A 9:16 vertical video, 25 seconds long, using **only the original audio** of the TikTok (@adriandanosaur, sound "Teach me how to dougie"). There are **no added sound effects**.

**The story:** a smooth-talking guy uses cheesy pickup lines on a girl at a coffee shop. She's not impressed, so he busts out the Dougie, and by the end she's dancing with him.

**The look: hand-drawn animation.** Rough pencil and ink lines, marker colouring and paper texture. On my side:
- The drawing **"boils"** (lines wiggle slightly, like real hand-drawn frames), animated on twos at 12 drawings a second.
- I add doodled hearts, stars and motion lines that look drawn with a pen.
- Captions are in a handwritten marker font, with a highlighter swipe behind each word as it's spoken.
- **The dance is a flipbook:** you generate several poses and I flip between them exactly on the beat of the song.

- **Cast:**
  - **Marcus:** the smooth talker.
  - **Jade:** the girl he's wooing.
